Direction B — The Ticket

Leads with the numbers. One continuous stub on the dark field, torn down both edges. Tier stops being architecture and becomes a gold pill at the head of each row, which buys back every pixel A spent on headings — so this is the direction that holds a full Saturday.

Remembered by the right-hand column. Fixed width, tabular figures, Plex Mono: 3.0u over 2.0u over 1.5u, decimal under decimal, all the way down. That is the one thing an image can do that the text share explicitly cannot — src/lib/share-text.ts opens by conceding it (“no amount of padding will make columns line up”). If the point of shipping an image is to do something text can’t, this is the direction that makes the argument.
