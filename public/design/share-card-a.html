Direction A — The Gameday Program

Leads with tier structure. The tiers are the organising idea, so they get to be the architecture: a gold tick, the tier in Graduate, and a hairline running out to the margin — the section headings of a printed gameday program. You read the card by conviction first and clock second, which is the sort order (tier desc, then kickoff asc).

Remembered by the ruled headings. Density ~7 bets; a heading costs vertical space, so this is the least dense of the three. Tier expressed as a heading over a group.

Note the two hard cases, both real and both rendered: Miami (OH) at Western Michigan is the longest matchup string the product can produce, and the last row is a futures bet with no game — no logos, no kickoff, so it falls back to the monogram that TeamMark.tsx already uses and the time column reads NO KICKOFF rather than going blank.
