What actually changed Travel 1.5% → 5%, plus a small vertical term and a four-stop cycle, so the glow wanders instead of sliding along one axis. Scale barely moved (1.05 → 1.06) on purpose: the blur sits on the glow itself, so translating it is nearly free while scaling it forces the blur to redraw every frame.

The score flare

New. A score on a card you have money on lifts the glow, then lets it settle. Hit the button — watch the glow swell and fade, not the number.

Why only your cards Motion on this slate means you have money on it — it is one of three cues separating a verdict glow from a team glow. So the flare fires on a covering/losing/bubble card and never on a team-tinted one, even though the score itself still pops on every card. A pregame slate stays completely still, which is the accepted cost of keeping that rule.
