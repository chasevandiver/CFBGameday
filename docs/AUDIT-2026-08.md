Adversarial audit of every page, component, query, migration, job, and model path in this
repository, judged against the sites this product competes with for Saturday screen time:
ESPN, The Athletic, Action Network, DraftKings/FanDuel, CFBD, CBS, Covers.

Method: every source file in `src/`, `scripts/`, and `supabase/` was read in full; the test
suite (97 tests) and a production build were run. Findings cite `file:line`.

## 1. Executive Summary

The bones are genuinely good. The slate page and game cards are the strongest part of the
product — the cover strip, field strip, crew line, bet slip, and live urgency sorting are
better *pick'em-sweat* UX than anything ESPN or the books ship. The honesty layer (frozen
predictions, append-only ledger, CLV-as-arbiter) is a real differentiator with actual schema
enforcement behind it. The design system (broadcast dark, tabular numerals, team-color rails)
is distinctive and mostly coherent.

But the product does not yet survive contact with its own mission statement — "the single
site a user leaves open all day on Saturdays from Week 0 through the National Championship":

1. **The season ends in November.** Postseason games are never ingested
   (`cfbd.games` defaults to `seasonType: "regular"`, `scripts/sync-games.ts`), the week
   selector stops at 15, and `fetchCurrentSeasonWeek` clamps to 15. Conference championship
   week, bowls, and the entire College Football Playoff — the highest-engagement stretch of
   the sport — are unreachable. This alone disqualifies the mission.

2. **The "current week" is wrong most of the week.** Week rollover is computed as
   `floor((now − week0_start)/7d)` from a Saturday date in UTC (`src/lib/queries.ts:453`),
   so the site flips to the next week at **Friday 7:00pm CT, mid-slate**, and — worse — all
   of Tuesday-through-Friday research time is spent defaulted to *last* week's finals.
   Thursday MACtion for the upcoming week is invisible on the default view every single week.

3. **The model's totals are fake.** Every pricing path sets `offense = defense = overall/2`
   and `tempo = 70` (`scripts/lib/jobs-core.ts:276-281,504-509`, `build-preseason.ts:393`,
   `team/[id]/page.tsx:103`). Plug that into `priceGame` and the projected total is
   **exactly 57.0 for every game in the sport** (the HFA halves cancel; verify the algebra in
   `src/model/ratings.ts:262-266`). The "Model total", "over/under lean", projected scores,
   and the O/U leg of the model report card are decorative. For a product whose entire brand
   is "receipts culture," shipping a constant disguised as a projection is the most
   off-brand thing in the codebase. The Off/Def columns on `/ratings` are the same number
   twice for the same reason.

4. **The integrity layer has holes you can drive a truck through.** Any signed-in crew
   member can `UPDATE profiles SET is_admin = true` on their own row (the update policy has
   no column restriction, `0001_core_schema.sql:308`), can rewrite `result`, `clv`,
   `payout_units`, and `units` on their own bets (the "void own bets" policy permits any
   column, `:343`), and can set `line_at_pick` / `units` to arbitrary values on picks
   pre-kickoff via direct PostgREST calls. The unhideable ledger is hideable.

5. **Signed-out users get a half-broken site.** Migration 0011 made the site public, but
   `/api/slate` and `/api/ticker` still return 401 for anon (`src/app/api/slate/route.ts:13`).
   A logged-out visitor can't change weeks (empty state: "No games loaded"), never gets live
   refreshes, and never sees the ticker.

Beyond the five headliners, the spec's most differentiated features are still missing: the
reason-tag audit (spec §5.3's marquee), the watchability score (§7), line-movement flags vs
the model's side (§4), soft-market taxonomy on Edges (§5.1), futures tracking, a rankings
page, rivalry data (table exists, nothing reads it), and any postseason/preseason modes.

Verdict: a strong Saturday-afternoon toy for a signed-in crew of ten, two hard weeks away
from being a daily-driver, and a season away from the mission statement.

## 2. Overall Score: 58/100

Weighted toward the four use cases (pregame research, live tracking, pick'em, postgame
review). Live tracking is genuinely good; pregame research is thin; postgame review is
honest but shallow; pick'em works but is minimal. The season-coverage gap and integrity
holes cap the score hard.

## 3. UI Score: 74/100

The slate design system is the best thing here: coherent tokens, real typographic hierarchy
(Barlow Condensed scorebug / Archivo body / Plex Mono numerals), tasteful team-color usage,
good chips, good skeletons.
Deductions: `/ledger`, `/crew`, `/login`, and the admin panels still use the **legacy**
token set (`bg-gold`, `border-chalk/25`, `bg-field-deep`, square corners) and look like a
different, older site than `/slate` and `/game/[id]`. Receipts/Edges are in-between. Two
design generations are visibly shipping at once.

## 4. UX Score: 60/100

Strong: live urgency sort, cover strip, one-tap picks, bet slip, day tabs, sensible filters,
empty states. Weak: the default week is wrong all research week (see §1.2); no active-tab
indication in the nav (`AppNav.tsx` has zero current-page state — you literally can't tell
what page you're on); filters/sorts don't persist or share (nothing in the URL but `week`,
and `replaceState` breaks back-button); no sign-out and no profile management anywhere;
`title=`-only tooltips are dead on touch; the game detail page — the page you open *during*
a game — never updates without a manual refresh.

## 5. Mobile Score: 62/100

Mobile-first per spec, and the cards do collapse to one column well. But: odds cells are
28px tall (`h-7`) and star buttons ~17px — both far under the 44px touch target floor; the
7-tab nav + auth + theme toggle relies on horizontal scroll with no affordance; the fixed
bet slip (340px, `bottom-4 right-4`) overlaps the last card and ignores iOS safe areas; text
at 9–10.5px is pervasive; no PWA manifest despite the spec explicitly requiring
home-screen install (`public/` still contains create-next-app's `next.svg`/`vercel.svg`
placeholders and no icons).

## 6. Desktop Score: 66/100

The 4-column 2xl grid is a real command center and the sticky filter bar works. But desktop
gets nothing extra: no multi-pane layout (slate + focused game), no keyboard shortcuts (the
prompt's `?`-style power tools are absent entirely), no dense table mode, and the game page
is a mobile column stretched to 768px with acres of dead margin.

## 7. Accessibility Score: 48/100

Genuine effort exists (aria-labels on odds cells and win-prob bars, icon+text result chips
"never color alone", `prefers-reduced-motion` global kill switch, sr-only select labels,
visible focus on card links). But:
- Sub-11px type everywhere (9px section labels, 10px hints, 10.5px chips) at 30–40% alpha —
  `text-chalk/35` on `#191512` fails WCAG AA by a wide margin, and it's used for
  load-bearing content (records, kickoff times, crew fades).
- `role="tablist"`/`role="tab"` day tabs with no arrow-key handling, no `tabindex`
  management, no `tabpanel` — worse than plain buttons.
- No `aria-live` anywhere: scores, cover strips, and the ticker update silently for screen
  readers on the one day the site exists for.
- Sortable table headers lack `aria-sort`; hover-only `title` tooltips carry real content
  (rating component hints, edge magnitude); `<sup>` rank numbers are unlabeled.
- Whole-card link overlay + interior buttons produces a focus order that jumps into a card,
  out to the star, back into odds cells; there is no skip link.

## 8. Performance Score: 55/100

The client is fine; the data layer is the problem.
- `fetchSlateView` (`src/lib/queries.ts:113`) runs 12 parallel queries per request,
  including `line_snapshots.*` for every game of the week. With the burst poller writing
  every 10 minutes across 5+ books, a 60-game Saturday accumulates tens of thousands of
  snapshot rows — all shipped from Postgres to the server function **on every page load and
  every 30-second poll of every connected client**, to be reduced to one consensus number
  and a 24-point sparkline. This is the thing that falls over in November.
- Same function fetches the full season's picks for all users, all profiles, all ratings
  rows for the season (every week × 136 teams), all poll rankings — per poll tick.
- `/game/[id]` fires 9 queries plus a full-table `profiles` select and the entire season's
  poll table; `fetchTeamAtsSeason` re-fetches every line snapshot for every final either
  team has played, per page view.
- Everything is `force-dynamic` with zero caching — defensible for live data, wasteful for
  teams/ratings/receipts, which change weekly.
- No pagination anywhere; Receipts will render every game of the season in one document by
  December (~800 rows before postseason… which doesn't exist anyway, see §1.1).
Bright spots: parallelized queries, realtime-with-poll-heal design, visibility-gated
polling, per-game event timestamps to prevent stale-poll rollback (`SlateView.tsx:60-99` is
genuinely careful work).

## 9. Sports Product Score: 45/100

Versus the competition:
- **ESPN/CBS/Fox**: they have box scores, drive charts, player stats, standings, rankings
  pages, news, video. This site has none of those — not even a top-25 rankings page, despite
  syncing three polls into `poll_rankings` daily. Conference standings: absent.
- **Action Network/Covers**: they have line-movement history charts with timestamps, public
  betting %, CLV tracking, bet sync. This site has a 56px sparkline and (real) CLV — the CLV
  is competitive, the movement UX is not. Movement flags vs the model's side (spec §4) were
  never built.
- **DraftKings/FanDuel**: the odds-cell → slip interaction is honestly right at parity as an
  interaction pattern — the best "copy" in the app. But no alt lines, no player props, no
  live lines (live odds aren't captured at all mid-game).
- **CFBD**: as a ratings nerd site, `/ratings` shows overall plus a fake Off/Def split. SP+,
  FPI, and Elo are fetched by scripts and referenced by the consensus flag, yet **never
  displayed** — spec §2.4 explicitly promises "all four systems side by side on every game
  card." A power-ratings nerd has zero reason to prefer this over CFBD's own site today.
- **The Athletic**: nothing editorial except the per-team LLM verdicts (good!) and the
  three questions (good!) — both buried, with no hub that surfaces the week's narratives.

## 10. Pick'em Score: 50/100

Works: one-tap spread picks with line-at-pick snapshotting, kickoff lock via RLS, crew
visibility, live cover status, season leaderboard with units/ROI/CLV, results on cards.
Missing or broken:
- Over/under picks: fully supported by schema, grader, and live-status code — there is
  simply **no UI to make one** (`PickButtons.tsx` renders two spread buttons only).
- Units/confidence: `picks.units` exists and is summed by the leaderboard, but no UI sets
  it — while a malicious user can set it via API (see Bugs #4).
- No weekly standings, no weekly winners, no "who picked what this week" grid (a pick'em
  staple since 2005), no pick history page per user, no CLV shown per pick anywhere.
- The 3-picks/week minimum and ROI→CLV tiebreakers from the League Rules are not
  implemented (`crew/page.tsx:118-121` sorts by units first, and its comment misquotes the
  rule); the League Rules themselves are "displayed in-app" per spec — they are not, anywhere.
- No lock countdown, no "you have 0 picks this week" nudge, no edit-resnapshots-the-line
  warning before you tap.

## 11. Live Game Experience Score: 65/100

The best area. Cover strip + bubble tier + field strip + last play + crew line on cards is a
real, differentiated sweat machine, and the live→pregame→final sectioning with urgency sort
inside Live is exactly right. What keeps it from 85+:
- **The game detail page is static** (`game/[id]/page.tsx` — server-rendered, no realtime,
  no poll, doesn't even show `last_play`). The page you'd pop open for *the* game is the
  only live surface that doesn't move.
- Signed-out and the whole thing degrades (401s, §1.5).
- No red-zone/upset/score alerts of any kind, no notification hooks, no audio, nothing for
  the "10 games at once" user beyond scanning cards.
- Live win prob exists (nice little model, `src/model/live.ts`) but there's no game-flow
  chart of it, no history — it's a bar with no memory.
- The ticker hides pregame games until 4h out, shows no ranks, no possession, no spread.

# Screen-by-Screen Audit

### `/slate` (SlateView + GameCard)
- **Purpose**: the command center; default landing (/ redirects here).
- **Strengths**: card system (three states + featured + skeletons), urgency sort, day tabs,
  realtime + poll-heal, filters, summary strip, bet slip integration, empty states.
- **Weaknesses**: wrong default week Tue–Fri (§1.2); filter state evaporates on refresh;
  hidden for anon after any refetch (401); summary strip's "Model ATS" mixes frozen and
  unfrozen predictions (`weekModelRecord` uses whatever row is on the view); week selector
  omits Week 0 labeling and stops at 15; "Game of the Week" chip vanishes the moment any
  filter/sort is touched (surprising).
- **Missing**: Noon/Afternoon/Primetime/Late kickoff groupings (spec §7 — day tabs are not
  time slots); watchability score/sort (spec §7 defines the formula, never implemented);
  spread/total/ML display for live games (live odds gone from card entirely); TV-network
  logos; "pinned = starred teams" is invisible until you notice the reorder — no visual pin
  indicator on the card.
- **Confusing UX**: `MoveIndicator` colors movement green when the spread drops (toward
  home) and red toward away (`chips.tsx:105-119`) — a value judgment that means nothing
  (green ≠ good for *you* or the model); readers will assume it's "steam toward my pick."
- **A11y**: tab-role misuse; score updates not announced; odds cells under-sized.
- **Perf**: §8 — the 30s poll re-runs the 12-query pipeline per client.
- **Delight ops**: score-flash + pop is already there and good; add a subtle upset-alert
  treatment (unranked leading top-10) and a red-zone pulse on the field strip.

### `/game/[id]`
- **Purpose**: single-game hub: header, pick, market, model, weather, trends, questions.
- **Strengths**: broadcast header with possession ball, your-action chips, clean market
  table with open/model rows, honest "for fun" trends disclaimer, three questions.
- **Weaknesses**: **static** — no live updates (the fatal flaw); `last_play` never shown;
  no sparkline axis/timestamps (just a squiggle); hardcoded "CT" label
  (`page.tsx:259`) while the slate renders viewer-local time — the same kickoff shows
  different times on two pages for a non-CT user; no metadata (`generateMetadata`) so a
  shared link says "The CFB Slate" with no teams.
- **Missing** (spec §6 explicitly): tale of the tape, unit-vs-unit grid, all-four-systems
  numbers row (SP+/FPI/Elo), rivalry/trophy module (`rivalries` table is populated by
  nobody and read by nobody), venue/stadium info, projected score *distribution*, box
  score/drives for live/final, head-to-head history.
- **Bugs**: pick section renders for signed-out users as if they could pick (buttons
  active, then the server action fails with "Sign in to save your picks" — should render a
  sign-in prompt instead).

### `/edges`
- **Purpose**: this week's flagged disagreements with stakes.
- **Strengths**: clean rows, frozen/unfrozen honesty note, ¼-Kelly stake surfaced.
- **Weaknesses**: only spread edges (no totals — though with totals ≡ 57 that's for the
  best right now); no soft-market context (spec §5.1's whole taxonomy: G5, MACtion,
  September rosters, backup QBs); no historical edge performance strip ("flagged edges are
  31–24 this season" belongs here, not buried in Receipts); kickoff times hardcode "CT";
  no link to log the suggested stake as a bet (the one obvious cross-sell in the app).
- **Empty state**: good.

### `/ratings`
- **Purpose**: the model's table.
- **Strengths**: sortable columns, conference chips, movement arrows, churn/luck columns
  with honest tooltips, rank-stays-by-overall behavior.
- **Weaknesses**: Off/Def are `overall/2` twice (§1.3) — displaying them is worse than not
  having them; no sparklines (spec §7 promises them); no search; no SP+/FPI/Elo comparison
  columns; Δwk arrows use gold/orange (why not win/loss green/red like everywhere else?);
  no sticky header on a 136-row table; no per-team click-through affordance visible (rows
  aren't links — you must know to go to /teams).
- **A11y**: sort buttons lack `aria-sort`; tooltips hover-only.

### `/teams` and `/team/[id]`
- **Purpose**: directory + preseason team page (spec §3's "heart of the August product").
- **Strengths**: component breakdown with honesty notes, LLM verdict block, schedule with
  win bars and projected record, FCS tagging.
- **Weaknesses**: hardcoded `season_id 2026` in both pages (`teams/page.tsx:18`,
  `team/[id]/page.tsx:52-77`) — the multi-season product dies at the stroke of 2027;
  schedule win probs re-price with `overall/2` ratings ignoring the stored off/def and the
  frozen predictions; conference is filterable but there's no search among 136 teams; team
  page shows no record, no poll movement, no returning-production percentiles, no roster
  churn ledger, no QB block, no trenches report (spec §3 items 2–7 absent — acknowledged
  slip items, but the page presents as done); no ATS trend on the team page (it exists
  per-game only); no link back from a game to the two team pages.
- **Weird**: `/teams` lists only rated (FBS) teams, silently — an FCS opponent from a game
  card 404s nothing but has no page reachable.

### `/ledger`
- **Purpose**: personal bet log + season dashboard.
- **Strengths**: append-only with void-not-delete, live status chips on open bets, CLV
  column, links bets to games for auto-grading, clean 4-stat header.
- **Weaknesses**: legacy visual language (this page looks two design systems old); **the
  reason-tag audit — the spec's single most differentiating ledger feature (§5.3: W-L/ROI/CLV
  by tag, "most bettors have one profitable angle and four leaks") — does not exist**; no
  cumulative units curve (spec: "season dashboard … cumulative units curve"); no filters
  (week/tag/type/result); record stat drops pushes; `line_taken` renders raw with no +/-
  formatting (`{b.line_taken ?? "–"}` vs `fmtSpread` everywhere else); no CLV coloring; the
  BetForm is a wall of unlabeled placeholder-only inputs (placeholders vanish on focus —
  classic form a11y failure) and offers this week's games only (can't log last night's bet
  after midnight rollover); futures tracker (spec §5.3) absent.
- **Empty state**: plain but present.

### `/crew`
- **Purpose**: leaderboard + commissioner tools.
- **Strengths**: leaderboard with units/ROI/CLV; invite flow that mints one-tap links is
  genuinely clever; adjustments panel with confirm-gating for LLM proposals.
- **Weaknesses**: legacy visuals; no weekly view, weekly winners, or last-week column; no
  per-user pick history drill-down; no min-picks rule; sorts by units with a comment
  misciting the League Rules; League Rules not displayed in-app (spec: "displayed in-app"
  — rule ambiguity is the #1 argument source, and the rules live only in a git repo);
  everyone's *bets* summary ("everyone's season numbers show on the Crew page" per the
  ledger's own footer copy) is **not actually on the Crew page** — only pick'em records
  are. The footer promise is false.
- **Admin**: adjustments list doesn't show proposed-by/when, no game-scoped adjustments UI
  (schema supports `game_id`, UI always inserts null), remove has no confirmation.

### `/receipts`
- **Purpose**: frozen predictions + calibration — the honesty page.
- **Strengths**: real calibration stats with the 52.4% breakeven callout; frozen timestamp +
  model version displayed; ATS grading vs close-at-freeze clearly labeled.
- **Weaknesses**: fetches **every frozen prediction ever, all seasons, unpaginated**
  (`receipts/page.tsx:26-30` has no `season_id` filter) and groups by week — two seasons in,
  Week 1 of 2026 and Week 1 of 2027 will merge into one garbled table; no filters (edges
  only / by week / by team); rows aren't links to game pages; no calibration *curve* (the
  spec's "do 70% favorites win 70%?" begs for a reliability chart, and the dataviz
  opportunity is completely passed up); no CLV-of-flagged-plays stat (spec §4 report card);
  SU favorite definition (`home_win_prob >= 0.5` → home) mislabels road favorites as home
  picks in the SU record when win prob is exactly .5 — cosmetic but sloppy.

### `/login` + auth
- **Strengths**: magic-link flow with friendly failure states; invite-only messaging;
  session-forever policy fits the audience.
- **Weaknesses**: legacy visuals; no AppNav (dead end — logo isn't even a link home);
  the "invite-only" error detection greps `error.message` for "invite" which Supabase
  doesn't actually say for allowlist rejections (users will see raw API errors); **no
  sign-out exists anywhere in the product**; no profile page (display_name is whatever the
  trigger derived — likely your email local-part — forever; `favorite_team_ids` is dead
  schema that nothing reads or writes, while "favorites" shipped as localStorage stars that
  don't roam devices).

### `/slate/preview`
- Design-review page, fine to keep — but it's linked from nowhere, ships to production, and
  its BetSlip is live with `seasonId={0}`: pressing "Log bets" fires a real insert that
  fails on the FK and shows the user a raw Postgres error. Gate it or neuter the slip.

### Score ticker
- Nice touch; but 401 for anon (never renders), shows nothing pregame beyond 4h, no ranks,
  no possession dot, kickoff times rendered without the timezone the rest of the site
  carefully manages, and it pushes the sticky filter bar's `top-[49px]` assumption around —
  the slate's control bar overlaps the ticker region when the ticker is present (the
  control bar is `sticky top-[49px]` = nav height only; scroll with a live ticker and the
  ticker slides under it awkwardly rather than the two stacking).

# User-Type Reality Check

- **Casual fan**: sees a wall of numbers with no explainers; no scores-only mode; "Edge",
  "CLV", "¼ Kelly" unexplained anywhere (no glossary, no onboarding). Fails.
- **Hardcore fan**: no rankings page, no standings, no stats, no news. They'll keep ESPN
  open — which is the loss condition. Fails.
- **Bettor**: CLV tracking is genuinely best-in-class-honest; but no movement charts with
  time axes, no live lines, no alt markets, no bet-vs-close on the game page. Half-wins.
- **Pick'em player**: makes picks fast, sweats them beautifully, but can't see the weekly
  grid or history. Half-wins.
- **Power-rating nerd**: fake Off/Def, no SP+/FPI/Elo display, no rating history chart.
  Fails hard — and this is the persona most aligned with the spec.
- **DFS player**: zero player data. Fails (arguably out of scope — then say so).
- **One-team follower**: stars roam per-device only; no team schedule alerts; team page has
  no news/verdict updates in-season. Weak.
- **10-games-at-once watcher**: the strongest persona — urgency sort, cover strips, ticker.
  Wins, if signed in.
- **Mobile / desktop**: see scores §5/§6.

# Full-Season Journey

- **Preseason**: team pages + verdicts exist (good start); no preview hub, no win-total
  market page, no portal/coaching/recruiting surfaces (spec §9 says editorial-by-design —
  but no editorial surface exists either). Freeze-at-kickoff of preseason pages (spec §3)
  not implemented.
- **Mon–Fri cycle**: no homepage-by-day (spec §7 — Mon results / Wed lines / Sat slate);
  the default week bug actively fights the weekly rhythm; no "top movers" view; no
  opening-line alerting; injury tracking is admin-only adjustments with no user-facing
  surface.
- **Saturday**: strong (see §11).
- **Sunday**: grading + ratings jobs run; but there's no "week in review" surface — recap,

# Technical Audit (condensed)

- **Dead/undead code**: `favorite_team_ids`, `rivalries`, `api_call_log` (client counts
  calls in-memory but nothing writes the table), `profiles.timezone`, `picks.units` (no UI),
  `RankBadge` (exported, unused), `AppNav.ready` flag (always true), `weather_gust/precip_in`
  fetched but unrendered.
- **Duplication**: consensus math exists twice (`lib/queries.ts` and `scripts/lib/jobs-core.ts`)
  with the jobs copy lacking the ML/open handling; abbr-fallback string munging appears 4×;
  edge-function is a verbatim fork of jobs-core (acknowledged, but drift is guaranteed —
  the two `consensus()` implementations already differ).
- **State**: the `useSyncExternalStore` stores are clean and correct; SlateView's stale-poll
  guard is excellent. `changeWeek` uses `replaceState` (breaks back), and clears+refetches
  even for already-loaded weeks (no cache).
- **Types**: hand-written db-types risk drift (`GameRow.status` is `string`, not the union);
  `PickRow.side` typed but `GameView.myPick.side` is `string`.
- **SEO/metadata**: no `metadataBase`, no OG/twitter cards, no per-page titles on half the
  routes, no dynamic titles on game/team pages, no JSON-LD `SportsEvent` (free rich
  results for "X vs Y" queries), placeholder favicons.
- **Testing**: model/lib coverage is real (97 tests, good ones — grading, live status,
  consensus); zero component/route tests; no CI workflow runs them (only `jobs.yml` exists
  — lint/test/build never run on push).
- **Ops**: GitHub Actions scheduler is a fine v1; edge-function secrets are string-replaced
  placeholders (`__JOBS_SECRET__`) — documented, but brittle; `freezeJob.currentWeek()`
  returns the *minimum* week with any scheduled game, so one postponed-and-rescheduled
  early-season game permanently pins the freeze job to that old week.
